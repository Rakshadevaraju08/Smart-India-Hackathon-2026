# Plinth Height Document Research Report

**Date:** 2026-09-20
**Objective:** Identify primary/authoritative documents containing exact plinth heights for the 20 critical substations used in Layer 4.

## Methodology
Extensive web searches were conducted targeting the Tamil Nadu eProcurement System (tntenders.gov.in), TNEB, TANTRANSCO, and TANGEDCO portals using combinations of substation names, voltage levels, and keywords like "plinth", "civil works", and "BOQ".

## Summary of Findings
Due to the structure of Tamil Nadu government procurement portals, actual engineering blueprints and detailed Bills of Quantities (BOQs) containing precise civil plinth elevations are locked inside archived tender PDF attachments that are not indexed by public search engines. 

As a result, no verified documents containing exact plinth heights were found.

## Conclusion & Next Steps
Public web search is insufficient for extracting specific civil engineering metrics for Tamil Nadu's critical electrical infrastructure. 

To obtain this data without violating the "No Estimation" rule, the project must either:
1. File a formal Right to Information (RTI) request with TANGEDCO/TANTRANSCO specifically requesting the "Transformer Plinth Elevation relative to MSL" for the 20 sites.
2. Obtain internal Approved for Construction (AFC) structural drawings directly from the Superintending Engineer (Civil) of the Chennai region.
