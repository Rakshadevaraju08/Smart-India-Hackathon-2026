# Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) - Solid Waste & Cleanliness Assessment Report

## 1. Executive Overview
- **Governing Body:** Greater Chennai Corporation (GCC) & Tamil Nadu Municipal Administration and Water Supply Department.
- **Geographical Scope:** 15 Administrative Zones (200 Municipal Wards), encompassing 426 sq km.
- **Estimated Daily Municipal Solid Waste (MSW) Generation:** **5,490 to 6,150 Metric Tonnes Per Day (TPD)**.
- **Average Per Capita Waste Generation:** ~0.65 to 0.72 kg/day.
- **Collection Coverage:** ~95.4% door-to-door collection using 2,885+ Battery-Operated Vehicles (BOVs) and 6,900+ compactor bins.

## 3. Concessionaire Operations Model
1. **Urbaser Sumeet (Private Concessionaire):**
   - Operates across **8 Zones**: Zone 7 (Ambattur), Zone 9 (Teynampet), Zone 10 (Kodambakkam), Zone 11 (Valasaravakkam), Zone 12 (Alandur), Zone 13 (Adyar), Zone 14 (Perungudi), Zone 15 (Sholinganallur).
   - Characterized by mechanized street sweeping, GPS/RFID-tracked BOVs, and containerized compactor trucks.
2. **GCC Departmental & Extended Regional Contracts:**
   - Operates across **7 Zones**: Zone 1 (Thiruvottiyur), Zone 2 (Manali), Zone 3 (Madhavaram), Zone 4 (Tondiarpet), Zone 5 (Royapuram), Zone 6 (Thiru-Vi-Ka Nagar), Zone 8 (Anna Nagar).
   - North Chennai zones face historical challenges with open dumping and legacy street littering.

## 4. Final Disposal & Legacy Dumpsites
- **Kodungaiyur Landfill (North Chennai):** Receives ~2,800 TPD from Zones 1 to 8. Ongoing biomining project to reclaim legacy waste acreage.
- **Perungudi Landfill (South Chennai):** Receives ~2,600 TPD from Zones 9 to 15. Located adjacent to the environmentally sensitive Pallikaranai marshland.

## 5. Swachh Survekshan Performance Analysis (MoHUA)
- **Swachh Survekshan 2023:** 
  - National Rank: **199th** (out of 446 cities).
  - Total Score: **4,313.79 out of 9,500** (45.41%).
  - Certifications: ODF++ certified, 1-Star Garbage Free City (GFC).
- **Swachh Survekshan 2024–2025:**
  - National Rank: **38th** (>10 Lakh ULB category).
  - State Rank: 104th across all ULBs in Tamil Nadu.
- **Key Bottlenecks Identified:**
  1. *Source Segregation Lag:* While collection coverage is 95%+, household source segregation remains at ~45-55%, causing unsegregated waste to choke transfer stations.
  2. *Drain Surface Cleanliness:* Score for cleanliness around stormwater drains and water bodies scored lowest (~41-45%), acting as the direct trigger for urban flash flooding.

## 6. Coupling Solid Waste to Urban Flood Nowcasting
1. **The Drainage-Solid Waste Feedback Loop:**
   - Dry plastic packaging, PET bottles, and styrofoam discarded on roads get swept into curb inlets during the first 15 minutes of a downpour (*First Flush Phenomenon*).
   - This creates an instantaneous reduction in curb inlet capture efficiency ($C_d$ drops by up to 60-80%), causing water to bypass the underground drains and accumulate along road medians.
2. **Zones with Critical Choke Risk:**
   - **Zone 5 (Royapuram - George Town Wholesale Market):** Heavy packaging material and corrugated boxes.
   - **Zone 4 (Tondiarpet) & Zone 6 (Thiru-Vi-Ka Nagar):** Narrow alleys, open silt catch pits, Otteri Nullah tributaries.
   - **Zone 9 (Teynampet - T. Nagar / Pondy Bazaar):** High footfall commercial shopping areas where roadside litter chokes Mambalam canal feeder drains.
